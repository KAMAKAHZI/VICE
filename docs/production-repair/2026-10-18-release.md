## Remaining dependencies and acceptance gaps

- Google Calendar: no published appointment schedule URL or authorized calendar/Zoom provider credentials are available. Root can configure the real Google booking page; its owner must connect the desired calendar and Zoom conferencing. Live availability is not fabricated.
- Marketplace: Inspirato has an official provider URL. Unconfirmed Exalted Fleet/concierge URLs require the actual company booking addresses.
- Trading: native MT5 compilation/execution, same-feed TradingView parity, real broker equity and order execution have not been verified. No order is submitted by personal-analysis enablement.
- Communications: ringtone/haptic logic is tested; physical iPhone/Android permissions, two-device calls, TURN/restrictive-NAT and screen sharing still require device tests. Group SFU, persistent receipts, editing/deletion and comprehensive message search remain unfinished.
- Documents/signatures: compatible PDF form editing and recorded attestations exist; complete provider-backed multi-signer requests/reminders and signed-PDF retention workflows remain unfinished.
- Real member authentication/recovery/invite, actual end-to-end Academy completion and SMTP delivery require authorized accounts/provider verification. Password-leak protection is still disabled in the Supabase project settings.
- Lending records document the agreement and reserve ledger units; they do not create lender approval, valuation acceptance, legal title or automatic enforcement.

Deployment and final registry correction results are appended after publication. The GitHub production smoke artifact and Netlify commit status provide release evidence independent of the version label.
